Contract Creation — the sub-status runner

The rail on top is the stage’s sub-statuses; the panel below plays one at a time. The card is the same height whether a stage has three sub-statuses or six. Here the same card is repeated with the panel forced onto each sub-status in turn, so all 41 are reviewable — in the app one plays, hides, and the next takes over.
